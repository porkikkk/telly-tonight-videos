Telly Tonight publisher: privacy policy

The Telly Tonight publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Telly Tonight channels to upload the channel's own videos to its own YouTube and Instagram accounts.

It accesses only those accounts, only to upload videos, set thumbnails and post comments. It does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other person. Access tokens are kept as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and can be revoked at any time in the Google and Meta account settings.
